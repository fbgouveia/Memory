## 4. Gelato — "Gelato accelerates fulfillment via agentic integration"

- URL: `https://crewai.com/case-studies/gelato-accelerates-fulfillment-via-agentic-integration`
- SHA-256: `ac9db14dde97526fe10388d7ba0123a15ffd9df482da50a402ee2d61a383e098`
- Interviewee: Styrbjörn Holmberg (Gelato).
- Indústria: On-Demand Manufacturing & Logistics Platform (Creator
  Economy).
- Setor: E-Commerce.
- Aplicações: bulk product-SKU data mapping, local print-on-demand
  fulfillment & logistics, carrier onboarding / supply-chain
  integration, internal workflow & efficiency automation.
- Quote: *"We can now integrate a new carrier in 10 minutes that used
  to take us 5 days."* — `commercial_claim`.
- Resultados declarados (`commercial_claim`):
  - SKU-mapping timelines cortados em >90%.
  - Carrier-integration effort cortado em ~99% (5 days → 10 minutes).
  - "without proportional increases in headcount".
- Contexto declarado:
  - 250 staff em 50 países.
  - Printers tinham que mapear manualmente até 200.000 SKUs (processo
    que levava 9–24 meses).
  - Agentes embutidos atrás de "Gelato Connect" (plataforma própria).
