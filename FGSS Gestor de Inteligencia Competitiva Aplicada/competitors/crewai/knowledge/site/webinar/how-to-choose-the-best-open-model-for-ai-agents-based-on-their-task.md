About
LLMs are the foundation of AI agents, but as LLMs improve in capabilities and the task complexity of AI agents increases, choosing the right model for individual agents can be challenging. In particular, open models such as DeepSeek provide an incredible opportuntiy to power agents with advanced reasoning at a lower costs and with faster inference.‍In this webinar, CrewAI and SambaNova will share how to evaluate open models for agents calling tools and generating content, their experience performing different tasks with different models and recommendations for selecting models based on agent tasks.
What you’ll learn
The latest open models, and their capabilities
How models impact the performance of AI agents
Choosing models based on agent tasks and output
Building agents in CrewAI with inference from SambaNova
How to monitor and manage LLM token costs
