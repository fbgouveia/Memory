About
The second webinar in our series on AI agent adoption focuses on the challenge of operating AI agents across an organization. It's one thing to deploy an agent. It's another to operate 100s of agents that spanning multiple business units, departments and teams.‍
We will discuss the full agent operations lifecycle from observation and optimization to management and scaling. This includes everything from integration, tracing and human-in-the-loop supervision to role-based permissions and usage monitoring.‍
