About
CrewAI's Agent Management Platform (AMP) enables enterprise to accelerate the adoption of AI agents at scale. In this hands-on workshop for both technical and business roles, attendees will learn how to get started with agentic systems.‍After a brief introduction and demo of CrewAI AMP, we will walk through exercises to build, deploy and run an agentic system with CrewAI Studio (no code). Then, we will use a shared notebook to walk through exercises that use CrewAI APIs to apply greater control.‍
