Here are some things you can do to get started:

Introduce yourself by adding your picture and information about yourself and your interests to your profile. What is one thing you’d like to be asked about?

Get to know the community by browsing discussions that are already happening here.

Be specific when creating a new topic.

  • Include all relevant details about your issue, such as your code and the full error traceback.
  • Avoid vague statements like “It doesn’t work” or “I’m not getting what I want”, as these don’t provide enough context for others to assist you.
  • Copy and paste content directly rather than sharing screenshots. This makes it easier for others to review and help.
  • Double-check your post to ensure it doesn’t include any API keys or sensitive information. Remove these before posting.

Show appreciation to community members.

  • If you’re the original poster of a topic and an answer resolves your issue, click the button to mark it as the solution. This helps others find useful answers quickly, as the solution will appear at the top of the topic in your first post.
  • If you’re not the original poster but find a post interesting, informative, or entertaining, click the button to show your appreciation or support!

Contribute by commenting, sharing your own perspective, asking questions, or offering feedback in the discussion. Before replying or starting new topics, please review the Community Guidelines.

Ask for help or suggest an improvement if you need assistance or have a suggestion. Feel free to ask in Site Feedback or contact the admins.
